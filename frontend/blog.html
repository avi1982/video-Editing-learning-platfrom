NOTES FOR CREATORS

Better edits start with better choices.

Short, practical ideas to help make your next video clearer, more intentional, and easier to finish.

EDITING BASICS · 4 MIN

Make every cut earn its place

Before adding an effect, ask what the cut helps the viewer understand or feel. Removing a pause can sharpen the pace; keeping one can give a moment room to land.

WORKFLOW · 5 MIN

A calmer timeline begins with organization

Name your clips, group related footage, and build a rough sequence before polishing. A little structure makes revisions quicker and keeps creative decisions easier to see.

MOTION DESIGN · 3 MIN

Use motion to guide attention

Animation feels purposeful when it helps the viewer follow the story. Start with clear timing and restrained movement, then add complexity only when it improves the message.
