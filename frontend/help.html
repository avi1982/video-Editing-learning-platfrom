Frequently asked questions

How do I save my learning progress?

Sign in to your EditMaster account before completing lessons. Your progress is tied to your account and appears on your dashboard.

How do I bookmark a lesson?

Open a course and select “☆ Save lesson” on any lesson card. Find your saved lessons on the dashboard.

Can I access the courses on a phone?

Yes. The course pages and learning dashboard adapt to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

Are the video editing apps included?

EditMaster teaches editing workflows. The editing applications are provided by their respective companies and may require separate downloads or subscriptions.
